## 2 Upload ke WaveDash

### **Cara Mudah (Manual):**

1. Buka [WaveDash Dashboard](https://wavedash.io/dashboard)
2. Login/create account
3. Klik "Create New Project"
4. Isi:
   - **Name**: joe-must-drive
   - **Display**: Joe Must Drive
   - **Desc**: Sidecar Zombie Survival FPS | Vibe Jam 2026
5. **Upload files** dari folder `dist/`:
   - Pilih SEMUA isi `dist/` (index.html, assets/, audio/, models/, dll)
   - Drag & drop atau klik button upload
6. Set entry point: `index.html`
7. Deploy! ⏳ Wait < 2 minutes

✅ DONE! URL Anda: `https://[project-name].wavedash.io`
